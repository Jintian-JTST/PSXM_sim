Least-Squares vs. Ideal-Conductor Solution

  • Chi2 (LS) method
    • discrete shield currents that null B on sample rings offset from the shield
  • Analytical method
    • continuous surface current from the multipole expansion, exactly cancels every exterior coil multipole
  • Result: LS converges to a stable 1.167× offset above the analytic curve
  • shield_n = 60 → 400: ratio 1.169 → 1.166 (flat, not converging to 1.0)
  • Confirmed independent of sample gap and sampling density
  • Conclusion: structural effect of discretizing a continuous shell, not a bug

Leakage at 0.419 m (Nearest-Beam Benchmark)

  • Physically-induced shield model (real copper, 1 μs pulse)
  • Unshielded: ~7 µT at 0.419 m
  • With induced shield: ~0.05 µT at 0.419 m
  • Suppression: ~140×
  • Bounded by real conductivity — the number to quote as a physical estimate
